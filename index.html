Hi! I am a PhD candidate in economics at Georgetown University. My research applies structural econometric methods to topics in labor, education, and sports. I am on the job market in 2026–27.

My job market paper, "Optimal Dynamic Selection Under Costly Evaluation: Evidence from Graduate Admissions," develops and estimates structural models of an admissions committee's dynamic selection problem and finds that reweighting several misweighted applicant characteristics would significantly increase the admitted cohort's average success probability.

My work on NFL quarterback prospect evaluation (with Alexandre Olbrecht and Aaron Schatz) has appeared annually on ESPN since 2021, and my research on professional tennis (with Axel Anderson, John Rust, and Kin-ping Wong) was published in the Journal of Political Economy in 2025.
